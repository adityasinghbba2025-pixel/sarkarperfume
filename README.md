# Scent Showcase Builder

● Get an introduction to AI coding agents (Codex) and how they connect to real repositories.

Step 1: Explore Sarkar Store and Pick Your Fragrance

1. Visit sarkar.store and look at how the brand presents its perfumes, packaging, pricing and tone of

voice.

2. Pick or invent your own fragrance name, notes (for example citrus, oud, floral, woody) and a one line

brand story.

3. Do not change the Sarkar packaging design. Your fragrance should look like it belongs on the same

shelf as the existing Sarkar products.

Doubt to resolve: confirm with your trainer whether the fragrance name needs to be original or can be

inspired by an existing global perfume, since this affects how you write the product description.

Step 2: Build Your Landing Page With Lovable

1. Open Lovable and start a new project for your fragrance landing page.

2. Include at minimum a hero section with the fragrance name and tagline, a section on fragrance notes,

a product image area, pricing or a call to action button, and a footer.

3. Keep the Sarkar packaging visuals intact if you are using product images. Focus your creativity on the

page layout, copywriting and offer, not the packaging.

Watch first: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vyrL7oiy1TQ

Step 3: Publish and Test With PageSpeed Insights

1. Publish your landing page from Lovable so it has a live link.

2. Go to pagespeed.web.dev and paste your live link to run the test.

3. Check both the Mobile and Desktop tabs. Most students will find more issues on the Mobile score.

Note down the specific warnings, for example large images, unused code, or slow loading elements.
